We came to Texas to be with friends, talk Linux, and have some good BBQ. We left with more friends and very satisfied bellies. This year’s Texas Linux Festival in Austin, TX did not disappoint.

The event was held on November 6 \- 7, 2026, at the Commons Event Center and Café on the J.J. Pickle Research campus. The Rocky Linux project had a table in the exhibit hall alongside many well-known projects and vendors.

![Road sign for the J.J. Pickle Research Campus in Austin](/images/content/2026-11-18-02-jj-pickle-research-campus.jpg)

**Teams represented**  
Several Rocky Linux teams were on hand: Patrick Roberts from the Infrastructure Team, Rebekah Roberts from the Community Team, and [Michael Young](https://rockylinux.org/news/2026-06-03-contributor-spotlight-michael-young) representing the Release Engineering and Community Teams. Our [Testing Team](https://docs.rockylinux.org/teams/testing/) Lead, Chris Stackpole (Stack), showed off our newly released testing framework and recruited everyone who stopped by to help with testing.

![The Rocky Linux booth set up in the Texas Linux Fest exhibit hall](/images/content/2026-11-18-03-rocky-linux-booth.jpg)

![Rocky Linux team members talking with a visitor at the booth](/images/content/2026-11-18-04-rocky-linux-team-at-booth.jpg)

**Testrarc**  
Besides grabbing some swag, visitors to our booth got a look at [Testrarc](https://testrarc.rockylinux.org), our newly released test management system. It is a web-based system designed specifically to track the progress of the testing of our next releases. For more details, check out our [our Testrarc announcement](https://rockylinux.org/news/2026-11-04-testrarc-announcement).

![Rocky Linux stickers, socks, and lanyards on the booth table](/images/content/2026-11-18-05-rocky-linux-swag.jpg)
![A Rocky Linux team member chatting with an attendee at the booth](/images/content/2026-11-18-06-booth-conversation.jpg)

**Lightning Talk**  
On Friday, during the lightning talk round, Stack gave a presentation on Testrarc. Despite some technical difficulties, he showed attendees how the community can jump in and help test the next release of Rocky Linux. Currently, we are working on [Rocky Linux 9.9 and 10.3](https://docs.rockylinux.org/teams/testing/documentation/qa_test_cases/). Whether you test on bare metal or in a VM, it counts, and your name can appear in the release credits. Come join the \`\#Testing\` channel at [chat.rockylinux.org](http://chat.rockylinux.org) to get started.

**Ready for next year**  
During the concluding comments on Saturday, the dates for next year were announced. Next year’s Texas Linux Festival will be held September 17 \- 18, 2027\. The website is already live, [2027.texaslinuxfest.org](http://2027.texaslinuxfest.org). We look forward to seeing y’all there\!
